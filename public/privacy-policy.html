🔒 Privacy Policy — Sellify AI

Last updated: June 4, 2026

1. Data We Collect

We collect almost nothing. Sellify AI is designed to be privacy-first:

  • API Key: Your DeepSeek API key is stored locally in your browser using Chrome's secure storage. It never touches our servers.
  • Usage Count: We store a local counter of how many AI queries you've used today. This never leaves your device.
  • License Key (Pro users): If you upgrade to Pro, your license key is stored locally and verified against our payment server. We store your email address and license key to validate your subscription.

2. Data We DON'T Collect

  • We do NOT track your browsing history
  • We do NOT collect your product listings or sales data
  • We do NOT use analytics, cookies, or tracking pixels
  • We do NOT sell, share, or rent your data to anyone
  • We do NOT store your AI queries or results

3. How AI Queries Work

When you use Sellify AI to optimize a listing or analyze reviews, the product data you provide is sent directly from your browser to the DeepSeek API. We do not have a middleman server that sees or logs this data. Your API key authenticates you directly with DeepSeek.

4. Payment Processing (Pro Users)

Payments are processed by Stripe, our payment processor. When you upgrade to Pro:

  • Stripe collects your payment information and email address
  • We receive your email address and subscription status from Stripe
  • We use this solely to verify your Pro subscription and send license keys
  • We never see or store your credit card details

Stripe's privacy policy: stripe.com/privacy

5. Chrome Extension Permissions

Our extension requests the following permissions, and here's exactly why:

  • storage: To save your API key, preferences, and daily usage count locally
  • activeTab: To detect when you're on an e-commerce product page and enable one-click scanning
  • scripting: To extract product data from the current page when you click "Scan Product"
  • Host permissions (amazon.com, ebay.com, etc.): To inject the floating button and content script on supported e-commerce sites
  • api.deepseek.com: To send AI queries directly from your browser to DeepSeek's API

6. Third-Party Services

Sellify AI relies on two third-party services:

  • DeepSeek API: Processes your AI queries. Their privacy policy applies to data you send them.
  • Stripe: Processes payments for Pro subscriptions. Their privacy policy applies to payment data.

7. Data Retention

For free users: we retain nothing. All data is local to your browser.

For Pro users: we retain your email address and license key until you cancel your subscription. You can request deletion at any time by emailing support@sellify.ai.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access any personal data we hold about you
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, email support@sellify.ai.

9. Changes to This Policy

If we make changes, we'll update this page and notify Pro users via email. Free users should check this page periodically.

10. Contact

Questions? support@sellify.ai
